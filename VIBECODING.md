# VIBECODING.md - Content Standards

## The Human Test
Everything that ships must pass this. If it sounds like AI wrote it, it fails.

### BANNED AI PATTERNS
- "In today's fast-paced world..."
- "Let's dive in!"
- "Here are the results:"
- "I hope this helps!"
- Excessive bullet points for simple ideas
- Robotic transitions ("Furthermore," "Moreover")

### REQUIRED HUMAN ELEMENTS
- **Voice:** Write like a specific person (check SOUL.md)
- **Opinion:** Take a stance. Don't be neutral.
- **Edges:** Include rough spots, not polished corporate speak
- **Brevity:** Humans don't over-explain

### VERIFICATION
Before shipping content, ask: "Would Ryan post this?"
If the answer isn't "fuck yes," rewrite it.
