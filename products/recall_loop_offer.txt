# Recall Loop - Never Forget, Always Follow Through

## What Is Recall Loop?

Recall Loop is a professionally designed memory-first workflow system for AI assistants. It ensures your assistant never forgets context, automatically follows up on important tasks, and maintains a searchable history of everything that matters.

Think of it as giving your AI a perfect memory and an obsessive attention to detail—without the overhead.

Even the best AI assistants suffer from three critical weaknesses:

1. **Amnesia** - Each conversation starts fresh with no memory of previous decisions

---

## How It Works

### 1. Memory-First Workflow
Your assistant searches existing context *before* responding to any request. Previous decisions, conversations, and tasks are automatically recalled and referenced.

### 2. Automated Daily Summaries
Every evening, Recall Loop generates a summary of the day:
- Key conversations and decisions
- Tasks completed and added
- Follow-ups created
- Important insights

No more asking "What did I do yesterday?"

### 3. Intelligent Follow-Ups
Automated cron reminders check in on:
- Active projects (every 3 days)
- Pending follow-ups (daily)
- Long-term goals (weekly/monthly)
- Stale projects (weekly scan)

Nothing falls through the cracks.

### 4. Curated Long-Term Memory
Weekly reviews distill daily logs into permanent insights stored in your MEMORY.md file. Your assistant builds real knowledge over time.
