# Zero-Capital Outreach Strategy

## Target Audiences

### 1. Crypto Investors
**Where to find them:**
- Twitter/X (follow #Solana, #Airdrop tags)
- Reddit (r/CryptoCurrency, r/Solana)
- Discord (crypto servers)

**Value prop:**
- Make $500-15K farming airdrops
- 15 min/day time commitment
- $150 starting capital

**Approach:**
- Comment on their posts first
- Add value, build relationship
- Soft pitch after 2-3 interactions

### 2. Developers
**Where to find them:**
- GitHub (Solana projects)
- Twitter/X (dev community)
- Discord (dev servers)

**Value prop:**
- Security audit for AI agents ($49)
- Airdrop farming guide ($9)
- Free tools (lead magnet)

**Approach:**
- Contribute to their open source
- Review their code
- Offer value first

### 3. Content Creators
**Where to find them:**
- YouTube (crypto channels)
- Twitter/X (influencers)
- Substack (crypto newsletters)

**Value prop:**
- Affiliate partnership (30% commission)
- Guest post opportunity
- Free product for review

**Approach:**
- Engage with their content
- Share their posts
- Propose collaboration

## Daily Outreach Goals (Zero Capital)

### Morning (30 min)
- 5 helpful Reddit comments
- 3 Twitter replies (add value)
- 2 GitHub contributions

### Afternoon (30 min)
- 5 personalized cold emails
- 3 Discord helpful messages
- 1 piece of content created

### Evening (30 min)
- Follow up on responses
- Review metrics
- Plan tomorrow

**Total:** 90 min/day

## Metrics to Track

- Emails sent
- Response rate
- Meetings booked
- Sales made
- Revenue generated
