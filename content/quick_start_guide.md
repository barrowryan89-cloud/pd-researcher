### What You're Farming For

Protocols track your activity and award points. When they launch tokens, points convert to tokens.

**Historical Examples:**
- Jupiter: 1 point ≈ $0.50-2.00 in JUP tokens
- Jito: Early stakers got 100-500 JTO tokens ($500-10,000)

### Risk Management

**Do:**
- Start small (0.25 SOL per wallet)
- Use different wallets for different protocols
- Check positions daily
- Withdraw if health factor drops

**Don't:**
- Deposit more than you can afford to lose
- Use leverage unless you understand it
- Ignore liquidation warnings
- Share your seed phrase
