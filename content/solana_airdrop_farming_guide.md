# How to Start Airdrop Farming on Solana: A Beginner's Guide

**Airdrop farming** is the practice of using decentralized protocols before they launch a token, in hopes of receiving a retroactive airdrop. In 2024-2025, Solana has become the hottest ecosystem for these opportunities.

## What You Need to Start

1. **A Solana wallet** (Phantom or Solflare recommended)
2. **Some SOL** for gas fees (0.5-1 SOL is enough to start)
3. **Time** — 10-15 minutes daily for farming activities

## Step 1: Get SOL

If you don't own SOL yet, you'll need to buy some. The easiest ways:

- **Coinbase** — Best for beginners, insured custody
- **Kraken** — Lower fees, good for active traders  
- **Binance** — Largest selection, global access

You'll need about $100-150 worth of SOL to farm effectively across multiple protocols.

## Step 2: Set Up Wallets

Create 3-4 separate wallets for different protocols. This helps with:
- Privacy (protocols can't easily link your activity)
- Organization (track which wallet farms which protocol)
- Risk management (if one wallet gets compromised, others are safe)

**Recommended setup:**
- Wallet 1: MarginFi (lending)
- Wallet 2: Kamino (yield farming)
- Wallet 3: Drift (perpetual trading)
- Wallet 4: Jupiter (DEX aggregator)

## Step 3: Start Farming

### MarginFi (High Confidence)
- **What:** Lending/borrowing protocol
- **Action:** Deposit SOL or USDC, optionally borrow against it
- **Points:** Live now at app.marginfi.com
- **Time:** 5 min daily to check positions

### Kamino (High Confidence)
- **What:** Automated yield vaults
- **Action:** Deposit into JitoSOL or mSOL vaults
- **Points:** Live now at app.kamino.finance
- **Time:** Check weekly

### Drift (High Confidence)
- **What:** Perpetual DEX
- **Action:** Deposit collateral, make small trades
- **Points:** Live now at app.drift.trade
- **Time:** 2-3 trades per week

### Jupiter (Medium Confidence)
- **What:** DEX aggregator
- **Action:** Swap tokens, use limit orders
- **History:** Already did JUP airdrop, may do more
- **Time:** Weekly swaps

## Expected Returns

**Conservative:** $500-2000 across all protocols (6 months)
**Optimistic:** $5000-15000 (if protocols value early users highly)
**Worst case:** Lose gas fees (~$100-150)

Past Solana airdrops:
- Jupiter: $1000-50000 per wallet

## Risks

1. **No guarantee of airdrops** — Protocols may never launch tokens
2. **Smart contract risk** — Protocols can be hacked
3. **Impermanent loss** — LP positions can lose value
4. **Time cost** — Daily/weekly maintenance required
