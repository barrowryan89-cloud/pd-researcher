## Risks

1. **No guarantee of airdrops** - Projects can change plans
2. **Smart contract risk** - DeFi protocols can be hacked
3. **Gas fees** - ~$150 over 6 months
4. **Time investment** - 40 hours total

## Advanced Strategies

### Multi-Wallet Farming
- 4+ wallets per protocol
- Different deposit amounts
- Spread across time

### Sybil Strategies
- Vary transaction patterns

- Unique IP addresses
