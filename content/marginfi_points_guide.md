# MarginFi Points Guide: How to Maximize Your Airdrop Eligibility

## What is MarginFi?

MarginFi is a decentralized lending protocol on Solana that allows users to:
- Lend assets to earn interest
- Borrow assets using deposits as collateral
- Earn "MRGN Points" for protocol activity

## How MarginFi Points Work

Points are awarded for:
- **Deposits** — 1 point per $1 deposited per day
- **Borrows** — 2 points per $1 borrowed per day
- **Referrals** — 10% of referred user's points

## Maximizing Your Points

### Strategy 1: Deposit + Borrow (The Loop)
1. Deposit 1 SOL
2. Borrow 0.5 SOL against it
3. Redeposit borrowed SOL
4. Repeat (careful of health factor)

**Risk:** Liquidation if SOL price drops

### Strategy 2: Stablecoin Farming
1. Deposit USDC (stable)
2. Borrow USDT (stable)
3. Redeposit USDT
4. Low volatility risk

**Points:** Lower but safer

### Strategy 3: Mixed Approach
- 50% in SOL deposits
- 30% in stablecoin loops
- 20% reserved for opportunistic borrows

## Health Factor Explained

**Formula:** (Collateral Value × Liquidation Threshold) / Borrow Value

**Safe Zone:** > 1.5  
**Caution Zone:** 1.2-1.5  
**Danger Zone:** < 1.2

**What happens if health factor < 1?**
Your position gets partially liquidated to repay debt.
