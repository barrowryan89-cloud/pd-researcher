    What Worked

    1. Consistency

  • Checked positions daily (5 min)
  • Never missed a week of Jupiter swaps
  • Maintained Drift activity even when boring

    2. Multiple Wallets

  • Separated protocols by wallet
  • Easier tracking
  • Privacy benefits

    3. Early Start

  • Started before most people knew about farming
  • Lower competition
  • More time for points to accumulate

    4. Risk Management

  • Never deposited more than gas money
  • Monitored health factors
  • Closed Drift positions before funding

    What Didn't Work

    1. Over-Trading on Drift

  • Took some losses trying to "farm harder"
  • Should have kept trades smaller
  • Lesson: Consistency > volume

    2. Ignoring Testnets

  • Missed some testnet-only airdrops
  • Zeta testnet participation could have qualified
  • Lesson: Do testnets too

    3. Not Documenting

  • Didn't track everything initially
  • Tax reporting was messy
  • Lesson: Log everything from day 1

    Key Lessons

    1. Start early — The earlier you farm, the more points you accumulate 2. Be consistent — Daily checks matter more than big one-time deposits 3. Use multiple wallets — Easier tracking + privacy 4. Don't overthink — Simple strategies beat complex ones 5. Document everything — You'll need it for taxes
